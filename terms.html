3. How Hereglow works

  • Turning on your glow. You can choose to "turn on your glow," which shows other Hereglow users whose glow is on within about 800 feet of you that you're single. Your glow turns off automatically after the time you choose (3 hours by default; 1 to 6 hours). You can turn it off sooner at any time. You can turn your glow on up to 4 times per hour.
  • Location. Hereglow uses your device's location while the app is open to find other users whose glow is on within about 800 feet of you. The distance is the same for everyone. Other users never see your exact location, distance, or direction. Location detection may be inaccurate, and we don't guarantee it will be correct or that your glow will be shown (or not shown) to any particular person.
  • Waves and matches. Lamplight subscribers who have verified their ID can view the profiles of others glowing within about 800 feet of them and send waves. Free (Glow) members see a count and blurred photos of others glowing within that distance. If a Lamplight member waves at you, you can wave back for free. Mutual waves create a match, and matched users can chat by text.
  • Discreet Mode. When Discreet Mode is on, Hereglow's push notifications say only "New wave" or "New message," with no name or message text, and the app hides chat previews and match pop-ups. It does not change the app's icon or name. It reduces, but does not eliminate, the chance that others will know you use Hereglow.

Free and paid features

  • Flares: one-time, consumable purchases. Each Flare highlights your glow for 30 minutes: people whose glow is on within about 800 feet of you see you first in their list, with a glowing ring.
