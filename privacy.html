The short version

  • Hereglow is for adults 18 and older only. Everyone must give a date of birth showing they are 18 or older. Lamplight (paid) members must also pass an ID and selfie check before they can view profiles or start waves.
  • When you choose to "turn on your glow," other Hereglow users whose glow is on, and was turned on within about 800 feet of where you turned yours on, can see that you're single. Your glow turns off automatically after the time you choose (default 3 hours, adjustable from 1 to 6 hours).
  • We use your device's location only while your glow is on, to find other people whose glow is on and was turned on within about 800 feet of where you turned yours on. While your glow is off, Hereglow doesn't send or store your location. We never show other users your exact location, how far away you are, or which direction you are in.
  • We don't keep a history of your location. While your glow is on, our server keeps two points for you, where you turned your glow on and your most recent position, both rounded to about 100 feet. Both are deleted as soon as your glow ends.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account and profile information: your first name, date of birth and age, up to 6 profile photos, a short bio, and who you're here to meet (dating, friendship, or not sure). Your first name, age, photos, bio, who you're here to meet, and whether your ID is verified are visible to other users as described below.
  • Sign-in information: your email address and, if you use Sign in with Apple (iPhone), your Apple sign-in identifier and the email Apple shares (which may be a private relay address). If you use Sign in with Google (Android), Google shares the email address on that Google account. If you sign in with an email and password, our authentication provider stores the password only in hashed form.
  • Verification information: before a Lamplight member can view profiles or start waves, our verification provider, Stripe, collects an image of the member's government-issued ID and a live selfie to confirm the member is 18 or older and that the selfie matches the ID. This may involve creating a face scan or face template to compare the selfie with the ID photo. Free (Glow) members are not asked to verify. See "Verification and biometric information" below.
  • Communications and activity: waves you send and receive, matches, chat messages (text only), and the content of anything you send us (for example, support requests).
  • Reports and blocks: if you report or block someone, or someone reports or blocks you, we keep a record of the report or block and any information provided with it. Reports, and blocks against a person, stay after an account is deleted, as described in "How long we keep your information."
  • Store offer or promo codes: if you redeem an Apple offer code, Apple handles the redemption through the App Store. If you later redeem a Google Play promo code, Google handles that redemption through Google Play. We learn only that you have Lamplight access, the same as for any other purchase.
  • Settings: your glow duration and whether Discreet Mode is on. Your notification and appearance choices are saved only on your device.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: with your permission, we use your device's GPS location while your glow is on to find other people whose glow is on and was turned on within about 800 feet of where you turned yours on. See "Location: how it works" below.
  • Device information: your push notification token, if you allow notifications (Apple's push service on iPhone, or Firebase Cloud Messaging, provided by Google, on Android). When the app connects to our servers, our hosting provider (Supabase) automatically logs your IP address and basic request details (such as the app and iOS or Android version the request came from) for security and troubleshooting.
  • Usage and diagnostics: we do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools in the app. Our servers record the actions the Service needs in order to work, such as waves, matches, and messages. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may share crash reports and aggregated app statistics with us. For apps installed from Google Play, Google may collect crash and performance data (Android Vitals), which we can view in Play Console.

Information from others

  • Apple and Google: when you buy a subscription or in-app purchase, the store you bought from (the App Store on iPhone, or Google Play on Android) confirms the purchase to the app, and the app tells our servers whether you currently have Lamplight access. When you buy Flares, the app sends our servers a signed or store-verified record of that purchase, which we check before adding the Flares to your account. We do not receive your payment card or billing details.
  • Verification provider: Stripe tells us whether a Lamplight member's verification passed and gives us the date of birth on the ID. We use that date only to confirm the member is 18 or older, and we do not store it.
  • Other users: information other users provide about you, such as reports, or messages they send you.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Run the Service: create and manage your account, show your profile to eligible users, turn on and expire your glow, deliver waves, matches, and messages, and provide Discreet Mode.
  • Find people near you: use your location to work out who else has their glow on and turned it on within about 800 feet of where you turned yours on, so we show your glow only to them.
  • Verify age and identity: confirm that Lamplight members are 18 or older and that their selfie matches their ID.
  • Keep people safe: moderate names, bios, chat messages, and photos (using automated screening tools, plus human review of user reports), enforce our rules—including our ban on photos of children—investigate reports, prevent fraud, spam, and fake accounts, and respond to emergencies.
  • Process purchases: unlock Lamplight subscription features, Flares, and Game Day Passes based on purchase status from Apple or Google Play.
  • Communicate with you: send service messages, safety notices, and (if you allow them) push notifications when someone waves at you or sends you a message. Normally, a wave notification shows the person's first name, and a message notification shows the sender's first name and the start of the message. With Discreet Mode on, notifications say only "New wave" or "New message."
  • Improve the Service: understand how the app is used, fix bugs, and develop features. Where practical, we use aggregated or de-identified data for this.
  • Comply with law: meet legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our Terms.

3. Location: how it works

Location is central to Hereglow, so we want to be specific.

  • When we collect it: only while your glow is on (starting when you turn it on) and the app is open and in use, and only if you grant location permission (Precise Location on iPhone, or precise location while the app is in use on Android). While your glow is off, Hereglow doesn't send or store your location. Hereglow does not request "Always" location access and does not track your location in the background.
  • What we do with it: our server compares the rounded point where you turned your glow on with the same point for other people whose glow is on, to find those whose starting point is within about 800 feet of yours. Your most recent position is used only to check whether you've moved more than about 800 feet from where you turned your glow on, which turns your glow off.
  • What other users see: only that your glow is on and was turned on within about 800 feet of where they turned theirs on. Other users never see your exact location, a map pin, your distance or direction from them, or your location history.
  • Fixed 800-foot radius: your glow is visible only to other users whose glow is on and was turned on within about 800 feet of where you turned yours on, and you see only them. Because each glow can move up to about 800 feet from where it started before it turns off, two people who see each other may at times be farther apart than 800 feet. The radius is the same for everyone and can't be changed.
  • Auto-expiry: your glow turns off automatically after the period you choose (3 hours by default; you can choose 1 to 6 hours). It also turns off if the app sees that you've moved well away (about 800 feet) from where you turned it on. You can turn it off sooner at any time.
  • Retention: while your glow is on, Hereglow's server keeps two points for you: where you turned your glow on and your most recent position, both rounded to about 100 feet. Each new position replaces the previous one, so there's no location history. Both points are deleted as soon as your glow ends, whether you turn it off, the 1–6 hour timer runs out, you move more than about 800 feet from where you turned it on, you sign out, your account is hidden after reports, or you delete your account. No other user can read these points; others only learn that your glow started within about 800 feet of where theirs did. The record of when your glow started and ends is deleted at the same time. To prevent misuse, we also count how many times you turn your glow on within an hour (no more than 4 are allowed); that count is deleted once the hour is over.
  • Turning it off: the app asks for Precise Location once, and your glow won't turn on without it. You can turn off location access, or Precise Location, at any time in your device settings (iPhone Settings, or Android Settings). Without it, you won't be able to turn on your glow.

Where you turn on your glow—for example, at a church—could reveal sensitive information about you (such as religious affiliation). We do not keep a history of where you've turned on your glow, we do not use your location to build profiles of your beliefs, interests, or habits, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Verification and biometric information

To keep Hereglow adults-only and reduce fake profiles, Lamplight members must complete ID and selfie verification through Stripe before they can view profiles or start waves. Free (Glow) members are not asked to verify.

  • Stripe may extract information from your ID (such as your name, date of birth, and ID number) and may create a face template from your selfie to compare it against your ID photo.
  • We use this information only for age verification, identity and photo verification, fraud prevention, and safety. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • Hereglow stores only whether you passed, the date you were verified, and Stripe's reference number for the verification. We do not store your ID images, your selfie, any face template, or the date of birth from your ID. Once a check finishes, whether it passes or needs to be redone, we ask Stripe to delete the ID images, selfie, and information read from your ID; Stripe says this can take up to 4 days. If you start a new check, we also ask Stripe to delete any earlier unfinished check, and when you delete your account, we ask Stripe to delete your most recent check.
  • Biometric data (face templates) is held only by Stripe, not by Hereglow, and is deleted with the rest of the check as described above. In the rare case that Stripe confirms a check but we can't read the date of birth from it, we leave that check at Stripe until you start a new check or delete your account.
  • By completing verification, you consent to the collection and use of your verification and biometric information as described here.

5. What other users can see

  • Free (Glow) members whose glow is on see how many others have their glow on and turned it on within about 800 feet of where they turned theirs on and one blurred photo of each, with no name or profile. The blurred copy is made on our server, so the clear photo never reaches their phone.
  • Lamplight members who have verified their ID and have their glow on can view the profiles of others whose glow is on and was turned on within about 800 feet of where they turned theirs on (first name, age, photos, bio, who they're here to meet, and a Verified badge if verified) and can send waves.
  • Flares: if you light a Flare, people whose glow is on and was turned on within about 800 feet of where you turned yours on see you first in their list, with a glowing ring and a "Flare" label, for 30 minutes or until your glow ends.
  • Photos: other people see only photos that pass our automated check. While a photo is being checked, only you can see it, marked "In review." Before upload, the app resizes each photo and removes the location stored in the picture.
  • If a Lamplight member waves at you, you can open their profile and wave back for free. If you both wave, it's a match and you can chat.
  • Once you match, you and your match can keep seeing each other's profile and photos in your chat, even after either of you moves away or turns off your glow, until one of you blocks the other or deletes their account.
  • Discreet Mode: when it's on, Hereglow's push notifications say only "New wave" or "New message," with no name or message text, and the app hides chat previews and match pop-ups. It does not change the app's icon or name on your home screen. You can also turn notifications off or hide previews in your device's notification settings for Hereglow.
  • Anything you put in your profile, or send to another user, can be seen, saved, screenshotted, or shared by that user. Please share thoughtfully.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • With other users, as described in "What other users can see."
  • With service providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that limit their use of your data to providing services to us. These include:
    • Backend hosting, database, authentication, and server logs: Supabase
    • Age and ID verification for Lamplight members: Stripe
    • Automated content moderation (names, bios, chat messages, and photos are checked before others can see them): OpenAI
    • Push notifications: Apple Push Notification service (iPhone) and Firebase Cloud Messaging, provided by Google (Android)
    • Sign-in: Apple (Sign in with Apple, on iPhone) and Google (Sign in with Google, on Android)
    • Subscription management: Apple and Google
  • With Apple, which processes your App Store payments and refunds under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • With Google, which processes your Google Play payments and refunds, delivers Android push notifications, and (if you choose Sign in with Google) authenticates that sign-in, under Google's privacy policy.
  • For legal reasons: when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, subpoena, court order, or other legal process.
  • For safety: to protect the safety of any person, prevent fraud or abuse, or respond to an emergency—including reporting suspected child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement as required by law.
  • To enforce our rights: to enforce our Terms and protect our rights, property, and users.
  • Business transfers: if Hereglow is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that deal, subject to this Policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

We may share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the app at You > Account > Delete account. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile and glow are removed from the Service right away.
  • Your profile, photos (including blurred copies), waves, matches, chats, blocks you made, push tokens, and sign-in account are deleted from our live systems right away. Reports you made or received, and blocks other people made against you, are kept as described in "How long we keep your information," with only a one-way fingerprint of your sign-in instead of your account. We also ask Stripe to delete your most recent ID check. Backup copies are overwritten on the schedule above.
  • Your chats are deleted for both people, so your matches will no longer see your messages.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Lamplight subscription. If you subscribed on the App Store, cancel it in your Apple ID settings (iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions). If you subscribed on Google Play, cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions, or at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Deleting the app or your Hereglow account does not stop future charges.

You can also request deletion by emailing support@hereglow.app.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit, encryption at rest, access controls, and limiting staff access to those who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee the security of your information. If we learn of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and authorities as required by law, including Tennessee's breach notification law (Tenn. Code Ann. § 47-18-2107).

10. Children

Hereglow is strictly for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Everyone must enter a date of birth showing they are 18 or older, our servers reject any younger date, and Lamplight members must also pass ID verification. Photos of children are prohibited on profiles, and we use automated screening and review of user reports to enforce this. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will close the account and delete their information, except where we must keep it for safety or legal reasons. If you believe a minor is using Hereglow, please contact us at support@hereglow.app. Our Child Safety Standards describe how we prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and how to report a concern.

11. Your privacy rights and choices

Choices for everyone

  • Profile: edit or remove profile information at any time in the app.
  • Location: turn off access in your device settings (iPhone Settings, or Android Settings).
  • Glow: turn your glow off at any time; it also expires automatically.
  • Notifications: turn off push notifications in your device settings.
  • Blocking and reporting: block or report any user from their profile or your chat.
  • Account deletion: delete your account in the app.

Rights under U.S. state privacy laws

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under state privacy laws—such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA), the Tennessee Information Protection Act (TIPA), and similar laws in other states. Subject to exceptions in those laws, these rights may include the right to:

  • Know / access: confirm whether we process your personal information and get a copy of it.
  • Correct: ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete: ask us to delete your information.
  • Portability: get your information in a portable format.
  • Opt out: opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, and profiling in furtherance of decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. (We don't sell your information or use it for targeted advertising.)
  • Limit or consent to sensitive data use: some laws treat precise location, biometric data, and certain other information as "sensitive." We process sensitive data only with your consent (where required) and only to provide the Service and keep it safe. You can withdraw consent by turning off the related permission or deleting your account, though some features may no longer work.
  • Non-discrimination: we won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Tennessee residents: under the Tennessee Information Protection Act, you may confirm, access, correct, delete, and obtain a copy of your personal information, and opt out of sale, targeted advertising, and certain profiling. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or emailing support@hereglow.app with the subject "Privacy Appeal." We will respond to appeals within the time the law requires. If you're not satisfied with the outcome, you may contact the Tennessee Attorney General.

California residents: in the past 12 months, we have collected the categories of personal information described in Section 1 (identifiers, personal characteristics such as age, commercial information such as purchase status, internet or other electronic activity, geolocation data, sensitive personal information such as precise location and biometric/verification data, and user content) for the business purposes described in Section 2, and disclosed them to the categories of recipients described in Section 6. We have not sold or "shared" (for cross-context behavioral advertising) personal information, and we have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing information of anyone under 16.

How to make a request

Email support@hereglow.app (you can also tap Contact in the app, under You > Support). We'll need to verify your identity, usually by confirming you control the account. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows; we may ask the agent for proof of authorization. We'll respond within the time required by law (generally 45 days).
