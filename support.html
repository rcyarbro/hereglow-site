Support

Questions, problems or feedback? We're glad to help.

Contact us

Email support@hereglow.app. Include the email or phone number on your account and your device model if you're reporting a problem with the app.

Delete your account

  1. Open Hereglow on your iPhone.
  2. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and profile are removed from Hereglow.

If you can't get into the app, email support@hereglow.app from the address on your account, or tell us the phone number you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Lamplight subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To cancel, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel Hereglow. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Safety & reporting

If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

  • Block: from any profile or chat, tap the menu and choose Block. They won't be able to see you or contact you.
  • Report: from any profile or chat, choose Report and tell us what happened. We review reports and may remove or ban accounts.
  • Email us: for urgent safety concerns, or to report someone you can't reach in the app, write to support@hereglow.app with as much detail as you can.

Meeting safely

  • Your glow shows that you're at the venue, never your exact spot. Turn it off any time.
  • Keep first conversations in public, among other people.
  • Don't share your home address, financial information or your kids' details with someone you've just met.
  • Trust your gut. If something feels off, leave, block and report.
