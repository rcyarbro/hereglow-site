Child Safety Standards

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This page is the published Child Safety Standards for Hereglow, the dating app published by Thirty One Thirteen LLC. It covers the Hereglow iPhone app, the Hereglow Android app, and this website. Google Play requires dating apps to publish standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). These standards also apply on iPhone.

Hereglow is for adults only

Hereglow is strictly for adults 18 and older. We do not allow minors to use Hereglow, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

  • Everyone must enter a date of birth showing they are 18 or older. The app and our servers both refuse a younger date.
  • Lamplight (paid) members must also pass an ID and selfie check through Stripe before they can view profiles or send the first wave, to confirm they are 18 or older.
  • Photos of anyone under 18 — including a person's own children — are not allowed anywhere on Hereglow, including profiles. Photos are checked with automated tools before anyone else can see them, and we review user reports.
  • If we believe someone is under 18, we will close the account immediately and delete their information, except where we must keep limited records for safety or legal reasons, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation, including any content or conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers a child. That includes, for example:

  • Grooming a child for sexual exploitation
  • Sextorting a child
  • Trafficking of a child for sex
  • Sexualizing a minor, or imagery that depicts, encourages, or promotes the sexual abuse of children
  • Sharing, requesting, or creating child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including photos, videos, and computer-generated imagery

You also may not use Hereglow as a reason to approach, photograph, or interact with a child. These rules are part of our Terms of Use. Accounts that break them may be banned without notice.

How to report a concern

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number) first.

You can report a concern to us without leaving the Hereglow app:

  • In the app: from any profile or chat, choose Report and tell us what happened. If you think someone may be under 18, choose the reason Might be under 18. You can also Block that person.
  • By email: write to support@hereglow.app with as much detail as you can. You can do this even if you can't use the app.

A person reviews reports. We may remove content and restrict or ban accounts. More about blocking and reporting is on our Support page.

How we address child sexual abuse material (CSAM)

After we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM on Hereglow, we will take appropriate action in line with these standards and applicable law, including:

  • Removing the material from Hereglow
  • Banning the account
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law enforcement, as required by law

We may keep limited safety records after an account is deleted, as described in our Privacy Policy, so earlier reports and blocks can still be used to protect people.

Child safety laws

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including having a process in place to report confirmed CSAM to NCMEC or the relevant regional authority.
