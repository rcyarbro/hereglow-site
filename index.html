Meet where you already are

Skip the weeks of messaging someone you've never met. Hereglow works in the places your life already happens.

Turn on your glow

At your kid's practice, a game, the gym, an event or the bar, flip on your glow. Other singles who are glowing at the same place can see you're here. Nobody else can.

Only "here," never exactly where

Hereglow shows that someone is at this venue, not where they're standing. No maps, no pins, no distances.

Wave first, then talk

Send a wave. If they wave back, you can chat, or just walk over and say hi. It's hard to catfish someone who's standing in the same room.

Your glow fades by itself

Visibility turns off automatically, so you're never left showing after you've gone home. You can switch it off any time.

Lamplight

Lamplight is Hereglow's paid tier, with more ways to connect with the singles around you. Plans and pricing will be shown in the app at launch.

Built for safety from the start

  • 18+ and opt-in. Hereglow is only for single adults, and you're invisible unless you choose to glow.
  • Approximate presence only. Others see that you're at the same venue, never your exact location.
  • Automatic expiry. Your visibility ends on its own.
  • Block and report. Block anyone instantly, and report people who break the rules. We review reports.
